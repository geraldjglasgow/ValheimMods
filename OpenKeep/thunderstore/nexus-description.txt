===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Storage for Valheim, plus a few base tweaks: craft, build and feed stations from nearby chests, ships and carts; quick stack, sort and trash; salvage, batches, recipe search and a recipe tracker; a build camera; stack and chest sizes; choose your bed after death, quick respawns, fires and smelters fed from chests. Server synced, hot reloaded.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
Storage for Valheim, plus a few base tweaks.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Reach: craft, build and feed stations from nearby chests and the ship or cart you are using.
[*]Stow: quick stack, store all, top up, sort, route and trash, by button or hotkey.
[*]Crafting: Salvage tab, batches, craft speed, recipe search, favourites, grid views, recipe tracker.
[*]Stacks and Capacity: stack sizes, weights, chest and station sizes, contents on hover.
[*]Build Camera: build from a free camera near a crafting station (B with the hammer).
[*]Off by default: cart workbenches, chest contents signs, shared chests, Auto Tidy (stray items go to the chest they belong in) and Blueprints (save buildings, place them as construction sites from the hammer).
[*]Homestead: choose your bed after death, quick respawns, campfires on wood, honey rate, fires, smelters and pets fed from chests, night-only torches, quicker Rested, auto repair, a shorter world save freeze.
[*]Works with PackPanel (inventory) and Wayfare (portals, quick jumps); neither is required.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Needed on the server and every client. Install BepInExPack for Valheim, then put OpenKeep.dll in BepInEx/plugins (or use a mod manager).
[list]
[*]Lowering a stack size loses items above the new limit.
[*]Before removing the mod: split oversized stacks, empty enlarged chests' extra rows, switch off Signs and Torches Night Only, and visit your bases by day.
[*]Ashlands or the Fire world key: a campfire on wood sets the floor alight.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
BepInEx/config/milkyteam.openkeep.cfg and seven OpenKeep.*.yml files. Every setting is described in the file and applies without a restart; the server's values bind every player. Console: [b]openkeep help[/b].
