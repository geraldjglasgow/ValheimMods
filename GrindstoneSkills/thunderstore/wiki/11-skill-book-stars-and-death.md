# Skill Book, Stars And Death

## The skill book

The Skills window (inventory, Skills button) becomes a skill book. Click a skill to see its page: your level and progress, what it does at your level (bonuses included) and its perks, with the level each unlocks at. Hover a gold word for what it means. Every skill has a page, other mods' skills included. The numbers follow the server's settings.

## Starred items

Up to 0.12.1, dishes, wild picks, crops and seeds rolled 0 to 3 stars. **0.13.0 removed that:** new items have 0 stars and stack like the game's. Items starred earlier keep their stars:

- Stars show on the icon and tooltip; different stars stack apart; recipes accept any stars.
- A starred dish gives more health, stamina and eitr and stays at full strength longer (section 2).
- A starred mead base makes starred meads; the windmill keeps grain stars; starred bait gives more and bigger fish.
- Eggs still carry their hen's stars, and Prime Cuts (if on) gives starred meat.

Kitchen trash filters set above "all dishes" now throw away every dish ([Cooking Skill](wiki:Cooking Skill)). Settings marked "No effect" on the skill pages only shaped star rolls.

### 2 - Stars

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |

| One Star Duration Bonus | 10 | % longer at full strength. |
| Two Star Food Bonus | 20 | The same for 2 stars. |
| Two Star Duration Bonus | 20 | The same for 2 stars. |
| Three Star Food Bonus | 35 | The same for 3 stars. |
| Three Star Duration Bonus | 30 | The same for 3 stars. |

### 3 - Odds

No effect since 0.13.0: `Odds At Level 0` (90, 10, 0, 0), `25` (45, 40, 15, 0), `50` (15, 40, 35, 10), `75` (5, 20, 45, 30), `100` (0, 10, 40, 50), `130` (0, 0, 25, 75), and `Ingredient Levels Per Star` (10).

### 7 - Display

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stars On Icons | true | Show stars on item icons. Per player. |
| Ingredient Order | LowestStarsFirst | Which starred stacks recipes use first (or `HighestStarsFirst`). Per player. |

## Skill loss on death (6 - Death)

Applies to every skill, the game's and other mods' included.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill Loss On Death | 5 | % of every skill's level lost on death (game: 5; 0 keeps all). |
| Lose Progress On Death | true | Also lose progress toward the next level, as in the game. |

The world's death penalty setting still applies on top.
