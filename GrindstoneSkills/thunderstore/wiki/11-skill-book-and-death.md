# Skill Book And Death

## The skill book

The Skills window (inventory, Skills button) becomes a skill book. Click a skill to see its page: your level and progress, what it does at your level (bonuses included) and its perks, with the level each unlocks at. Hover a gold word for what it means. Every skill has a page, other mods' skills included. The numbers follow the server's settings.

## Skill loss on death (6 - Death)

Applies to every skill, the game's and other mods' included.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Skill Loss On Death | 5 | % of every skill's level lost on death (game: 5; 0 keeps all). |
| Lose Progress On Death | true | Also lose progress toward the next level, as in the game. |

The world's death penalty setting still applies on top.

## Display (7 - Display)

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stars On Icons | true | Show an egg's stars (its hen's level) on its icon. Per player. |
