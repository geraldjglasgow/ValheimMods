===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Shared parties for Valheim: a server-owned roster with one leader, invites, party-only chat, a draggable health panel, colored floating names, always-on map pins, friendly-fire protection, a party-only map ping, and a public API for other mods. Server synced where it matters, personal to each player everywhere else.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
Party adds real parties to Valheim: form a group with one leader, invite friends by name, and get shared chat, live health bars, always-visible map pins and friendly-fire protection that all work across a dedicated server, not just for a host. The server owns the roster, so a party survives logging out and a server restart; members are tracked by their persistent player ID, never by name, so two players who share a name are never confused.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install BepInExPack for Valheim.
[*]Install Party. On a server, install it on the server to have it enforce the gameplay settings; every client needs it too.
[/list]
For manual install, drop Party.dll into BepInEx/plugins.

[size=4][b]Commands[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]/party invite <name>[/b] (or /invite) - invites an online player; creates your party and makes you its leader if you have none yet. Any member may invite.
[*][b]/party leave[/b] (or /leave) - leaves your current party.
[*][b]/party remove <name>[/b] (or /remove) - leader only; removes that member.
[*][b]/party promote <name>[/b] (or /promote) - leader only; hands leadership to that member.
[*][b]/party p [text][/b] (or /p) - with text, sends a party-only message; with no text, toggles party-chat mode.
[*][b]/party panel edit[/b] / [b]done[/b] - drag the health panel, then lock it back in place.
[/list]
Short forms register only if another mod hasn't already claimed the word; /party ... always works. Every command answers you in chat, success or failure. /invite tab-completes against online players; /remove and /promote against your own party.

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Server-owned roster: one leader, a configurable size cap (default 8), automatic leadership succession when the leader leaves.
[*]Invites are a prompt the other player accepts or declines, with a timeout; either way you learn the outcome.
[*]Party chat reaches every online member regardless of distance, in your own chosen color, with a toggle mode and an on-screen indicator.
[*]A draggable, fully configurable health panel: position, scale, opacity, bar size, row spacing, font, and optional stamina/eitr bars, all personal to you.
[*]Ailment icons under each member's bars - burning, spirit fire, poison, frost, lightning, smoked, tarred, freezing, cold and wet - using the game's own status icons; switchable off.
[*]Floating names of nearby party members draw in your party color, leader marked distinctly.
[*]Party members always show on your map and minimap in your party color, even with their own position sharing off - this only applies within your party.
[*]Friendly-fire protection, on by default, covering melee, projectiles and area damage alike, without affecting creatures, falls, fire or drowning.
[*]Hold a modifier key while pinging the map to ping only your party, drawn in your party color and marked private.
[*]A public API for other mods to check party membership, fetch members, and subscribe to changes, without a hard dependency.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Server settings[/b][/size]
Gameplay settings - party size, friendly-fire protection, invite timeout - come from the server and can be locked so clients can't override them, the default. Display settings - colors, panel layout, the ping key - are always personal to each player and never pushed.

[/list]
