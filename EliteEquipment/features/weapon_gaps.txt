Valheim Weapon Asset Gaps

Updated October 10, 2026

Asset backlog for vanilla Valheim and the current Elite mod lineup. The sections list missing biome upgrades and unfinished integrations across all requested weapon and shield families. Bronze crossbow remains an additional Black Forest option; the bow list contains only the Plains gap.

Tiers run from 1 Meadows, 2 Black Forest, 3 Swamp and 4 Mountains to 5 Plains, 6 Mistlands, 7 Ashlands and 8 Deep North. New material names are proposed asset directions. A gap means no dedicated upgrade for that biome; earlier gear remains usable. Training items, disabled recipes and enchantment variants do not add tiers. Public mod examples are references, not installed coverage.
