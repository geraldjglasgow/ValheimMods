Weapon gaps
Updated 2026-10-10

Tiers: 1 Meadows, 2 Black Forest, 3 Swamp, 4 Mountains, 5 Plains, 6 Mistlands, 7 Ashlands, 8 Deep North.
A gap is a biome with no dedicated item. Material names are proposals.


CROSSBOWS
  Add:  1 Wooden crossbow
        2 Bronze crossbow (beside the Bone Crossbow)
        3 Iron crossbow
        4 Silver crossbow
        5 Blackmetal crossbow
  Have: 2 Bone Crossbow, 6 Arbalest, 7 Ripper, 8 Nord
  Note: Gravebranch is art only, not a playable item.

FLETCHING TABLE
  New crafting station for bows, crossbows, arrows, bolts and ballista missiles.
  Ballistas themselves are placed with the Hammer.


GREATHAMMERS
  Add:  1 Stone greathammer
        4 Silver or crystal greathammer
        5 Blackmetal greathammer
        7 Flametal greathammer
  Have: 2 Stagbreaker, 3 Iron Sledge, 6 Demolisher, 8 Nord

PICKAXES
  Add:  4 Silver pickaxe
        7 Flametal pickaxe
        8 Bloodgold pickaxe
  Have: 1 Antler, 2 Bronze, 3 Iron, 6 Blackmetal (needs Yggdrasil wood)

BALLISTA
  Add:  1 Wooden, 3 Iron, 4 Silver, 5 Blackmetal, 7 Flametal, 8 Bloodgold ballista
  Test: 2 Bone Ballista (ECP, built, in-game test open)
  Have: 6 vanilla Ballista
  Note: Bone Ballista is manual, vanilla is automatic. Control and ammo for new tiers need design.

MAGIC STAFFS
  Add:  1 Wood or flint, 2 Bronze or bone, 3 Ancient bark or iron, 4 Silver or crystal, 5 Blackmetal staff
  Have: 6 Embers/Frost/Protection/Dead Raiser, 7 Fracturing/Wild/Dundr/Trollstav, 8 Lightning/Vengeance/Echo Spike/Spirit Caller
  Note: each needs a spell, school and balance. Early staffs need an eitr source (caster gear).

WANDS
  Test: 1 Gnarled Wand (model and AssetLab prototype, off by default)
  Add:  2 Greywood, 3 Rootbound, 4 Frostsilver, 5 Blackmetal, 6 Yggdrasil, 7 Ashwood wand (names approved)
        8 Deep North wand (name, model and spells need design)
  Have: none in vanilla


Sources: installed vanilla item and recipe data, Elite Creatures Pack, EliteEquipment and AssetLab records.
