# EliteEquipment

More from the game's own equipment. First up: boots, split from every leggings in the game and worn on the feet.

## Features
- Boots: each of the game's 20 leggings becomes trousers and matching boots.
- Stats split 80/20: armour, armour per upgrade, weight and stat bonuses; every pair of boots adds 5% movement speed.
- Sets: the boots belong to the leggings' set, so a full set is one piece more.
- Recipes split the same way, crafted beside the leggings at the same station; early boots cost more.
- The game's own look: leggings end at the ankle and the boots are the footwear cut from them.
- Rag Shoes: painted cloth foot-wraps for the rags, which have no footwear of their own.
- Fitted leggings: Iron Greaves become mail leggings, Bronze Plate Leggings bronze plates on leather, Wolf Hide
  Trousers silver mail with white fur, Carapace Greaves scales with chitin knee cuffs, Flametal Greaves flametal mail
  with heat-tinted knee plates, Trousers of the Protector dark leather with steel flame bands and Trousers of the Vanguard
  teal cloth with gold vine diamonds to the ankle, in the look of their chests, fitted to the wearer so no skin shows
  through when you run.
- The Protector breastplate's plates sit close over the fitted trousers, and its front tabard swings like cloth.
- PackPanel: a Feet slot under Legs.
- EliteCrafting: inscriptions on worn boots count; boots have their leggings' item level.
- Works with mods that change item weights, such as OpenKeep's Stacks.

## Install
Needed on the server and every client. Install with r2modman or the Thunderstore app, or put `EliteEquipment.dll` in
`BepInEx/plugins`. Uninstalling removes the boots you made; the leggings go back to the game's own.

## Configuration
`BepInEx/config/milkyteam.eliteequipment.cfg`. `Separate Boots` (on) switches the boots on or off; off, worn boots come
off and the leggings are the game's own again. The server's values bind every player (`Lock Configuration`). Console:
`charter status` shows whether the server binds your settings.
