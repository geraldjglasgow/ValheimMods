# EliteEquipment

More from the game's equipment: boots split from every leggings, the shields and weapons the game is missing, and a
healer and caster class.

## Features
- Boots: each of the game's 20 leggings becomes trousers and matching boots, stats and cost split 80/20; boots add 5%
  movement speed and stay part of their set.
- Fitted leggings: Iron, Bronze, Wolf, Carapace, Flametal, Protector and Vanguard trousers fitted to the wearer, so no
  skin shows through when you run.
- Shields: bucklers for the biomes that had none, a Bronze round shield and two tower shields.
- Weapons: 22 new weapons filling the gaps in the game's lines, crafted beside the game's own of the same tier.
- New icons for the game's own shields and for the weapons in these lines, drawn to match.
- Casters (off by default): wands that heal friends and fire a charged bolt, a focus, seer's robes for every biome,
  spells learned from scrolls and eitr foods.
- PackPanel: a Feet slot under Legs. EliteCrafting: inscriptions on worn boots count.
- Works with mods that change item weights, such as OpenKeep's Stacks.

## Boots
Rag Shoes, Leather, Troll Leather, Bronze Plate, Bear, Iron, Root, Fenris, Wolf Hide, Vilebone, Lox Fur, Padded,
Carapace, Eitr-weave, Ask, Flametal, Embla, Protector, Caller and Vanguard boots or shoes.

## Shields
- Wood Buckler (Meadows), Silver Buckler (Mountains, a silver wolf head), Black Metal Buckler (Plains), Flametal Buckler
  (Ashlands): quick parries.
- Bronze Shield: the Black Forest's round shield.
- Silver and Carapace Tower Shields.

## Weapons
- Greatswords: Bronze, Iron, Silver, Black Metal.
- Dual axes: Bronze, Iron, Silver, Black Metal, Carapace, Bloodgold.
- Fists: Flint Knuckles, Iron and Flametal Claws.
- Daggers: Iron, Flametal. Atgeirs: Silver, Flametal. Axes: Silver, Flametal.
- Flametal Greataxe, Black Metal Bow, Black Metal Spear.

## Casters
- Wands: Gnarled Wand, Greywood Wand. Left mouse fires a bolt (hold to charge), right mouse casts your two prepared
  spells, dodging blinks.
- Raven Fetish: an off-hand focus for stronger heals.
- Seer's robes: Deerhide, Mossweave, Rootweave, Wolfwool, Sunweave, Mistweave and Emberweave, each with eitr.
- Spells: Mend (heal a friend or yourself), Hearthsong and Stag's Swiftness (buffs for everyone near; Eikthyr drops
  Swiftness's scroll). Prepared in the Spellbook.
- Eitr foods: Dandelion Mash, Candied Thistle, Glowcap Broth, Seer's Porridge, Turnip Crisps, Blood Draught, Mire Stew,
  Glacier Tea, Wolfheart Stew, Candied Cloudberries, Lox Marrow Broth, Sunbarley Porridge.

## Install
Needed on the server and every client. Install with r2modman or the Thunderstore app, or put `EliteEquipment.dll` in
`BepInEx/plugins`. Uninstalling removes everything made with it; the leggings go back to the game's own.

## Configuration
`BepInEx/config/milkyteam.eliteequipment.cfg`: one switch per section, `Separate Boots`, `Shields`, `Weapons` (on) and
`Wands and Spells` (off). Every setting is described in the file and applies without a restart; the server's values bind
every player (`Lock Configuration`). Console: `charter status` shows whether the server binds your settings.
Translate: save a copy of [English.yml](https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteEquipment/EliteEquipment/translations/English.yml) as `EliteEquipment.translations.<Language>.yml` in `BepInEx/config`.
