# Shields

Section `2. Shields`, one Server setting, on by default.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Shields` | on | Adds the shields the game has none of for a biome, and gives the game's own shields new icons. |

## What changes

- The game has a buckler for the Black Forest, Swamp, Mistlands and Deep North and none for the other biomes. This adds a buckler for the Meadows, Mountains, Plains and Ashlands, the Black Forest's round shield, and tower shields for the Mountains and Mistlands.
- Each shield is crafted at the station and level of the biome's shield it sits beside. Numbers are for an unupgraded shield; the cost in brackets is added per upgrade level.
- Every shield gains 6 block, 50 durability and 5 deflection a level (the Wood Buckler gains no deflection), and lowers your movement speed by 5% (tower shields by 10%).
- Shields you have already made stay and still work after you switch the setting off, but they cannot be crafted any more.

| Shield | Biome | Station | Block | Deflection | Weight | Durability | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood Buckler | Meadows | Workbench 1 | 4 | 20 | 3 | 200 | Wood 8 (+4), Leather scraps 4 (+2), Resin 2 (+1) |
| Bronze Shield | Black Forest | Forge 1 | 30 | 30 (+5) | 5 | 200 | Wood 10 (+5), Bronze 8 (+4) |
| Silver Buckler | Mountains | Forge 3 | 45 | 30 (+5) | 4 | 200 | Silver 10 (+5), Fine wood 4 (+1) |
| Silver Tower Shield | Mountains | Forge 3 | 78 | 125 (+5) | 5 | 200 | Fine wood 15 (+10), Silver 10 (+5) |
| Black Metal Buckler | Plains | Forge 3 | 61 | 40 (+5) | 4 | 200 | Black metal 10 (+5), Fine wood 4 (+1), Chain 2 (+1) |
| Carapace Tower Shield | Mistlands | Black forge 1 | 122 | 150 (+5) | 5 | 250 | Carapace 25 (+12), Scale hide 5 (+3), Eitr 10 (+3) |
| Flametal Buckler | Ashlands | Black forge 3 | 83 | 40 (+5) | 4 | 300 | Flametal 10 (+5), Blackwood 4 (+1), Asksvin hide 2 (+1) |

Deflection is the parry bonus the shield adds to a timed block.

## The kinds

| Kind | Shields | Parry bonus | Movement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buckler | Wood, Silver, Black Metal and Flametal Buckler | 2.5x | -5% |
| Round shield | Bronze Shield | 1.5x | -5% |
| Tower shield | Silver and Carapace Tower Shield | none | -10% |

- A buckler blocks less than a shield and parries far better.
- A tower shield cannot parry and slows you more, but stops almost anything.

## Icons

- While `Shields` is on, the game's own bucklers and round shields get new icons, drawn the same way as the new shields. Switch it off and the game's icons come back.
