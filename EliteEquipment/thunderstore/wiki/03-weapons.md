# Weapons

Section `3. Weapons`, one Server setting, on by default.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Weapons` | on | Adds 22 weapons that fill the gaps in the game's weapon lines, and gives the game's weapons of those kinds new icons. |

## What changes

- Each new weapon plays like a game weapon: the same moves, attack animations, stamina use, speed and trail. Only its model, name, damage and recipe are new.
- Each is crafted at the station and level of a game weapon of its own tier. The cost in brackets is added per upgrade level.
- Weapons you have already made stay and still work after you switch the setting off, but they cannot be crafted any more.
- While `Weapons` is on, the game's own weapons of these kinds (greatswords, dual axes, fists, knives, atgeirs, axes, greataxes, bows and spears) get new icons, drawn the same way as the new weapons. Switch it off and the game's icons come back.
