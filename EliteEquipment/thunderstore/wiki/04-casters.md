# Casters

Section `4. Casters`, one Server setting, off by default.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Wands and Spells` | off | A healer and caster line: wands that cast spells, an off-hand focus, seer's robes for every biome and eitr foods from the Meadows to the Plains. |

## What off means

- With `Wands and Spells` off, none of it can be crafted, Eikthyr drops no spell scroll, spell scrolls teach nothing, wands cast nothing and fire no bolt, worn gear gives no eitr, and there is no spell bar, Spellbook or spell tooltip line.
- Items you have already made stay in your inventory.
- Switch it on and the game's own eitr is added to from the start: worn seer's robes, a wielded wand and the eitr foods give eitr before the Mistlands.
- Required on the server and every client.

## Controls

Anyone holding a wand is a caster. A wand is one-handed, so the other hand can hold a focus, a shield or a torch.

- **Left mouse:** fires the wand's bolt for 4 eitr (up to a third less with Elemental Magic). Quick clicks chain a three-move combo like a sword's swings.
- **Hold left mouse:** charges the bolt. The longer you hold, the stronger and bigger it gets, up to +30% damage at 3 seconds. Let go to throw it. You walk slowly and turn to aim while charging, and the charge costs no extra eitr.
- **Right mouse (let go):** casts the spell in slot 1.
- **Right mouse held + left mouse:** casts the spell in slot 2 at once. Letting go of right mouse then casts nothing.
- **Right mouse held + jump:** dodges without casting.
- **Middle mouse:** casts slot 1.
- **Dodge with a wand drawn:** a short blink instead of a roll, up to 5 m, stopped short of anything solid. It costs the roll's stamina and half that in eitr, has a 0.8 second cooldown, and nothing a roll would dodge hits you from the blink until half a second after.
- A caster does not block: the block button does nothing with a wand drawn.

## Spellbook

- Each wand holds 2 spells ready. A new wand starts with Mend in its first slot.
- With the inventory open and a wand in hand, a Spellbook button appears. Click a slot, then click a spell you know. Clicking the picked slot again empties it.
- The spells are saved on the wand, so two wands on the hotbar are two loadouts.
- While a wand is drawn, a spell bar under the hotbar shows both slots with each spell's icon, eitr cost and button.

## Learning spells

- Mend is known by every character from the start.
- Every other spell is learned from a spell scroll: use it from the inventory. A spell you already know cannot be used, so a scroll can be handed to a friend.

| Scroll | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Spell: Hearthsong | Crafted at the workbench: Raspberries 5, Honey 2, Dandelion 3 |
| Spell: Stag's Swiftness | Dropped by Eikthyr, one for each player present |

## Spells

Spell costs are cut by up to a third with Blood Magic. Heals go to the friend you aim near within 30 m (other players and your tames); a gold marker shows who. Your character turns to them as you cast.

| Spell | Eitr | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mend | 8 | Heals the marked friend, or yourself if none is marked, by the wand's heal power |
| Hearthsong | 15 | Chant: everyone within 10 m, you too, regains health 50% faster for 60 seconds |
| Stag's Swiftness | 15 | Chant: everyone within 10 m, you too, moves 10% faster and runs on 20% less stamina for 60 seconds |

## Wands

Heal power rises by 1.5 a quality. Bolt damage is the pierce and the spirit amount each, and spirit hurts skeletons, draugr and ghosts more. Both weigh 1 and have 100 durability. The eitr is gained while the wand is wielded.
