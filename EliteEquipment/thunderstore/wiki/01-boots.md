# Boots

Section `1. Boots`, one Server setting, on by default.

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Separate Boots` | on | Splits every leggings of the game into leggings and boots worn on the feet. |

## What changes

- Each of the game's 20 leggings gets matching boots: Iron Greaves and Iron Boots, Trousers of Ask and Boots of Ask, Rag Trousers and Rag Shoes, and so on.
- The leggings keep 80% of their armour, armour per upgrade, weight, stat changes and eitr regen. The boots carry the other 20%.
- Every pair of boots gives +5% movement speed, whatever its leggings do.
- Resistances and other effects stay on the leggings. Both pieces keep full durability.
- The boots belong to the leggings' set. A full set needs one more piece than before: the old full set plus its boots.
- The recipe is split the same way: the boots take a fifth of each material, rounded, and the leggings keep the rest. Upgrades are split the same way.
- Boots cost more than their fifth: Rag, Leather, Troll Leather, Bronze, Bear, Root, Vilebone and Lox boots cost three times as much to craft, Padded boots twice, and every boots material costs at least 2, to craft and per upgrade. As in the game, each upgrade costs more than the last (the per-upgrade amount once, twice, then four times).
- Upgrade kits are not split: the leggings and the boots each need the leggings' kit at an upgrade station.
- The boots are crafted at the leggings' station and level.
- Leggings now end at the ankle, and the boots are the game's own footwear from those leggings, on your feet. Everyone sees what you wear.
- Leather and troll leather boots are painted on, as the game paints them. Rag Shoes are painted cloth foot-wraps.
- Iron Greaves are mail leggings in the iron chest's mail, with a leather belt and iron buckle and a strap above and below each knee. They are made to fit your own body, so no skin shows through when you walk or run. They reach the ankle, with a leather cuff; with boots on they end inside the boots.
- Bronze Plate Leggings are the bronze chest's plates on dark leather from the hips to the ankles, with a bronze trim above each knee and a bronze disc as the buckle. They fit your body the same way.
- Wolf Hide Trousers are the wolf chest's silver mail from the hips to the ankles, a little thicker than the iron mail, with the chest's pale fur under the belt and round each knee, a leather belt with a silver buckle and a strap over each knee's fur. They fit your body the same way and stay full length with boots on: the boots go over them. Without boots they end in a leather ankle cuff.
- Carapace Greaves are the carapace chest's dark round scales from the hips to the ankles, with a blue chitin cuff round each knee from the plates the boots wear, a leather belt with a steel buckle and a leather ankle cuff without boots. They fit your body and go under the boots like the Wolf Hide Trousers.
- Flametal Greaves are the flametal chest's heat-tinted mail from the hips to the ankles, with a cuff of its heat-tinted plate round each knee, a mauve leather belt with a steel buckle and a leather ankle cuff without boots. They fit your body and go under the boots the same way.
- The Trousers of the Caller, of Ask and of Embla and the Eitr-weave Trousers are painted on, as the game paints them, carried down to the ankles (the Caller's criss-cross, Ask's cloth, Embla's mail, the Eitr-weave's laced cloth).
- Trousers of the Protector are their own dark leather from the hips to the ankles, with a band of the breastplate's steel and orange flames above each knee, edged in ember orange, a leather belt with a steel buckle and a pale fur ankle cuff without boots. They fit your body and go under the boots the same way. Over them the Protector breastplate's plates sit close to the hips (looser behind, so they hang off the backs of the legs) and follow your legs as you walk, and its front tabard is shorter and swings like a cape, pushed by your thighs. The back tabard is left off.
- Trousers of the Vanguard are their own teal cloth tapering to the ankles, its gold vine running down the front and back of each leg in diamonds from the thighs to the ankles, with a gold braid belt and a gold brooch buckle, and a gold braid ankle cuff without boots. They fit your body and go under the boots the same way.
- Lox Fur Trousers keep their fur down to the ankles; with the Lox Fur Boots on you see the game's whole leggings.
- Mods that change item weights (such as OpenKeep's Stacks) still apply: the 80/20 split is taken from whatever the leggings weigh.

## Wearing boots

- Right click boots to put them on. Right click again, or drag them out, to take them off.
- With PackPanel, boots go into the Feet slot under Legs.
- With EliteCrafting, inscriptions on worn boots count, and boots have their leggings' item level, so they roll inscriptions as strong as the set's.
- Boots wear down from hits like any armour, and they come off when they break.
- When `Separate Boots` is switched off, worn boots come off and the leggings are the game's own again. Boots you have made stay in your inventory but cannot be worn until it is switched back on.
- Uninstalling the mod removes the boots you made.
