# Changelog

## 0.4.2

- Russian translation updated.

## 0.4.1

- Russian translation, used while the game's language is Russian.

## 0.4.0

- Translations: every word you read is in `translations/English.yml`; an `EliteEquipment.translations.<Language>.yml` in `BepInEx/config` translates it.

## 0.3.2

- Fixed: players on a slow connection with many mods are no longer refused when joining a server.

## 0.3.1

- Fixed: the Gnarled Wand, Spell: Hearthsong and Dandelion Mash can be crafted at the workbench (`Wands and Spells` on).

## 0.3.0

- Shields: Wood, Silver, Black Metal and Flametal Bucklers, a Bronze Shield, Silver and Carapace Tower Shields (`Shields`).
- Weapons: 22 new greatswords, dual axes, claws, daggers, atgeirs, axes, a greataxe, a bow and a spear (`Weapons`).
- New icons for the game's own shields and for its weapons in those lines.
- Casters: wands, a focus, seer's robes for every biome, three spells and twelve eitr foods (`Wands and Spells`, off by default).
- Seer's trousers split into trousers and boots like every leggings.

## 0.2.0

- Boots: leggings from other mods can bring their own boots to split into.

## 0.1.0

- First release: boots moved here from OpenKeep, which no longer has them.
- The game's 20 leggings split into trousers and boots (80/20 stats and cost); boots give +5% speed.
- `Separate Boots` is on by default; PackPanel gives boots a Feet slot.
- Trousers redrawn to reach the ankles, many fitted to the wearer so no skin shows through; new icons.
- Works with mods that change item weights; with EliteCrafting, boots have their leggings' item level.
