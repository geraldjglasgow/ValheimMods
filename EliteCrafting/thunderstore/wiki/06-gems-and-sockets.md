# Gems and Sockets

Weapons, staves, armour, shields and backpacks (with PackPanel) can carry up to three **sockets**, and a **gem** set into a socket adds a stat to the item. Tools, trinkets, utility items and torches take no sockets.

## Sockets

- The **Dvergr Chisel** cuts one to three sockets into a weapon, staff, armour piece, shield or backpack that has none, of any rarity, crafted items included: on Normal and Magic items one 75%, two 20%, three 5%; on Rare items one 62.5%, two 27.5%, three 10%. Each socket shows in the tooltip as an "Empty socket" line and as a small circle in the icon's lower right corner (a set gem shows its colour there). An item that already has sockets never gets more.
- Cleansing, Recasting and the other inscription runes leave sockets and gems alone. A sealed item takes no gem and no chisel.

## Setting a gem

Click the gem onto the item in your inventory, the way you use a rune, or use the Rune Table's **Sockets** tab.

- The gem fills the next empty socket. Its stat depends on the item: one for weapons (melee, bows and crossbows), one for staves, one for armour, shields and backpacks.
- Its tier is rolled when you set it, from the tiers the item's level has unlocked, weaker tiers more often, as an inscription rolls. A gem in Meadows gear rolls low; in Ashlands gear it can reach T1.
- A gem's stat adds to the same inscription already on the item and counts toward the same caps, but not toward the rarity's prefix and suffix limits.
- With every socket full, you pick the gem to replace: a question for each socket in turn ("Socket 2 holds Thor's Gem: +6 lightning damage. Replace it?"); Yes replaces that one, No asks about the next. The old gem is lost. At the Rune Table, pick the socket in the Sockets row first.

## Where they drop

- Gems are a boss reward: only bosses drop them.
- Every creature can drop a Dvergr Chisel in place of a rune: 10% of the runes in the Meadows, up to 17% in the Deep North (about one kill in 29 in the Meadows, one in 8 in the Deep North); stars raise the odds as they do for runes.
- Every boss can drop any of the gems, at random: 50% for one gem, plus 50% for one more for each star the boss has (a 4-star boss can drop five); and the Dvergr Chisel 25% of the time.
- World chests can hold a chisel too, as one of their runes.

## The Rune Table's Sockets tab

Your gear that takes sockets is listed on the left. Pick one, then the Dvergr Chisel or a gem the table holds; the text says what it would do on this item (the chisel: its odds for one, two or three sockets) and the tier range it can roll. The Sockets row shows the item's sockets: click a filled one to replace its gem. **Set gem** (or **Cut socket**) uses one the table holds. The table stores them like runes: **Store all**, or **Ctrl + click** one in your inventory, puts them in; **Shift + click** one in the row asks how many and takes them out. Gems and chisels you carry work the normal way too: click one onto the item.

## Setting

`Gems and sockets` in `2 - Runes` (on by default, follows the server). Off: the chisel and the gems neither drop nor work, the Rune Table hides its Sockets tab; gems already set keep their stats.
