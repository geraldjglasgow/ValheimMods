# Gems and Sockets

Weapons, staves, armour and shields can carry up to three **sockets**, and a **gem** set into a socket adds a stat to the item. Tools, trinkets, utility items and torches take no sockets.

## Sockets

- The **Consecrated Rune** gives a weapon, staff, armour piece or shield that has no sockets one (70%), two (25%) or three (5%), of any rarity; they show in its tooltip as "Empty socket" lines.
- The **Dvergr Chisel** cuts exactly one socket into an item that has none, of any rarity, crafted items included. An item that already has sockets never gets more.
- Cleansing, Recasting and the other inscription runes leave sockets and gems alone. A sealed item takes no gem and no chisel.

## Setting a gem

Click the gem onto the item in your inventory, the way you use a rune, or use the Rune Table's **Sockets** tab.

- The gem fills the next empty socket. Its stat depends on the item: one for weapons (melee, bows and crossbows), one for staves, one for armour and shields.
- Its tier is rolled when you set it, from the tiers the item's level has unlocked, weaker tiers more often, as an inscription rolls. A gem in Meadows gear rolls low; in Ashlands gear it can reach T1.
- A gem's stat adds to the same inscription already on the item and counts toward the same caps, but not toward the rarity's prefix and suffix limits.
- With every socket full, you pick the gem to replace: a question for each socket in turn ("Socket 2 holds Thor's Gem: +6 lightning damage. Replace it?"); Yes replaces that one, No asks about the next. The old gem is lost. At the Rune Table, pick the socket in the Sockets row first.

## Where they drop

- Every creature can drop a gem or a Dvergr Chisel in place of a rune, very rarely: a gem about one kill in 2,500 in the Meadows, one in 640 in the Deep North; the chisel about as often. Stars raise the odds as they do for runes.
- Every boss drops the Dvergr Chisel 25% of the time, and its own gems (table above).
- World chests can hold them too, as one of their runes.

## The Rune Table's Sockets tab

Your gear that takes sockets is listed on the left. Pick one, then the Dvergr Chisel or a gem you carry; the text says what it would do on this item and the tier range it can roll. The Sockets row shows the item's sockets: click a filled one to replace its gem. **Set gem** (or **Cut socket**) uses one from your inventory; the table does not store gems.

## Setting

`Gems and sockets` in `2 - Runes` (on by default, follows the server). Off: the Consecrated Rune, the chisel and the gems neither drop nor work, the Rune Table hides its Sockets tab; gems already set keep their stats.
