===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Runes and magic gear in three rarities: Normal, Magic and Rare. Creatures, bosses and chests drop runes; click a rune onto an item to awaken, recast, raise or cleanse it, cut sockets for gems, or gamble and seal it with the Serpent Rune. Six runes, a Rune Table, 209 inscriptions, eleven gems. Server synced. Required on the server and every client.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
[b]Work in progress:[/b] significant changes will come, including to the config, the YAML files and item data.

Runes and magic gear: creatures and chests drop runes; a rune clicked onto an item changes its rarity, inscriptions or sockets.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Rarities: Normal, Magic and Rare, the name and icon in the rarity's colour (below).
[*]Inscriptions: 209 magic properties; each kind of item rolls its own (below).
[*]Tiers: gear from later biomes rolls stronger inscriptions; T1 is the strongest.
[*]Runes: six, clicked onto an item from your inventory or an open chest (below).
[*]Rune Table: stores runes, turns trophies into essence; an essence (Beast, Stone, Elemental, Spirit, Grave) chooses what Ascension adds.
[*]Sockets: up to three, cut by the Consecrated Rune or one by the Dvergr Chisel, filled with eleven very rare gems (below).
[*]Drops: creatures, bosses and world chests drop runes, more with stars; magic gear never drops.
[*]Salvage: with OpenKeep, salvaged Magic or Rare gear may give a rune back.
[*]Magic items on the ground glow in their colour under a beam of light.
[*]Elite Creatures Reborn: Synergy (off by default) lets elite stars raise drops.
[*]An API for other mods: PackPanel's backpacks and Elite Creatures Pack's creatures and bone weapons join in.
[*]Every rune, inscription, command and file: [url=https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteCrafting/CLAUDE.md#player-reference]CLAUDE.md on GitHub[/url].
[/list]

[size=4][b]Rarities[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Normal: a plain item, no inscriptions.
[*]Magic (green): two inscriptions, one prefix and one suffix.
[*]Rare (blue): three inscriptions, at most two prefixes and two suffixes.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Runes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Awakening: makes a Normal item Magic with two inscriptions.
[*]Recasting: replaces both inscriptions on a Magic item with two new ones.
[*]Ascension: makes a Magic item Rare, keeping both and adding a third.
[*]Consecrated: one to three sockets on a weapon, staff, armour piece or shield that has none.
[*]Cleansing: strips a Magic or Rare item back to Normal.
[*]Serpent: a gamble (nothing, a 3rd inscription on Magic or a 4th on Rare, or a wild reroll), then sealed for good.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Gems[/b][/size]
A gem's stat depends on the item (weapon, staff, or armour and shield); its tier is rolled for the item when set.
With every socket full you pick which gem to replace.
[list]
[*]Surtr's, Ymir's, Thor's, Nidhogg's, Hel's: fire, frost, lightning, poison or spirit damage, a staff bonus, resistance.
[*]Tyr's: physical damage, summon damage, less stagger.
[*]Freyja's, Odin's, Skadi's: health, eitr or stamina leech, a staff bonus, more of it.
[*]Heimdall's: critical hits, cast speed, avoiding hits.
[*]Sleipnir's: movement speed, armour and shields only.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Inscriptions[/b][/size]
A prefix is the item's core power (damage, health, armour, blocking, leech); a suffix is everything else.
[list]
[*]Weapon damage (18): more damage, critical hits, slayers.
[*]Elemental brands (6): flat fire, frost, lightning, poison or spirit damage.
[*]On hit (10): chain lightning, paralysis, stagger, hamstring, sneak attacks.
[*]Attack speed and cost (10): faster, longer, wider, cheaper swings.
[*]Leech (6): health, eitr or stamina from hits and kills.
[*]Ranged and magic (12): draw and reload speed, Volley, Twincast, summons.
[*]Throwing (3): throw a melee weapon, recall it, leap to it.
[*]Blocking and parry (14): stronger blocks and parries, avoiding hits, thorns.
[*]Health, stamina and eitr (14): more of each, faster regeneration.
[*]Armour and resistances (25): armour, less damage by type, less stagger.
[*]Movement (23): run, sprint and swim faster, double jump, glide, sail.
[*]Weather, food and meads (16): never Cold or Wet, longer food and meads, a light.
[*]Fortune and insight (15): carry more; more runes, coins and trophies.
[*]Gathering and building (15): extra ore, wood, meat and harvest; free builds.
[*]Skill masteries (18): extra skill levels.
[*]Item properties (4): durability, weight.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Needed on the server and every client, same version. Install BepInExPack for Valheim, then put EliteCrafting.dll in BepInEx/plugins (or use a mod manager). Uninstalling deletes every rune and gem; magic gear turns plain until reinstalled. Mods that make weapons or armor stackable can merge magic items and lose inscriptions.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
BepInEx/config/com.EliteCrafting.cfg and the EliteCrafting_*.yml files. Every setting is described in the file and applies without a restart; the server's values bind every player (Lock Configuration). Console: [b]ecraft help[/b].
