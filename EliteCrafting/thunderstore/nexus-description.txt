===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Crafting stones and magic gear across six rarities. Creatures and chests drop stones and rarity-colored gear; click a stone onto an item to raise its rarity or add, swap, reroll, lock or corrupt its affixes. 27 stones, 16 biome essences, 162 affixes, salvage into shards. Server synced. Required on the server and every client.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
Crafting stones and magic gear: creatures and chests drop both, and a stone clicked onto an item changes its rarity or affixes.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Six rarities, Common to Mythic, by affix count; magic gear glows on the ground.
[*]162 affixes for every kind of gear, stronger the later the item's biome.
[*]27 stones: click one onto an item to promote, reroll, add, remove, lock, corrupt or copy it; a refused stone is kept.
[*]16 biome essences reroll an item around their family; sigils steer the next stone.
[*]Salvage: Shift + End grinds a magic item into shards; five shards fuse a stone.
[*]Drops from kills (more with stars, sure from bosses) and world chests.
[*]Elite Creatures Reborn: Synergy (off by default) lets elite stars raise drops.
[*]OpenKeep's Salvage tab leaves magic items to this mod's Salvage.
[*]Every stone, essence, affix, command and file: [url=https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteCrafting/CLAUDE.md#player-reference]CLAUDE.md on GitHub[/url].
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Needed on the server and every client, same version. Install BepInExPack for Valheim, then put EliteCrafting.dll in BepInEx/plugins (or use a mod manager). Uninstalling deletes every stone, essence and shard; magic gear turns plain until reinstalled. Mods that make weapons or armor stackable can merge magic items and lose affixes.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
BepInEx/config/com.EliteCrafting.cfg and the EliteCrafting_*.yml files. Every setting is described in the file and applies without a restart; the server's values bind every player (Lock Configuration). Console: [b]ecraft help[/b].
