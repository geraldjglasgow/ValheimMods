# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- All 27 stones work, Unmaking, Serpent, Binding, Chance, Reflection, sigils, Honing and Tempering included.
- 16 biome essences reroll items around their family.
- Shift + End salvages magic items into shards; five fuse a stone.
- World chests hold stones and magic gear.
- 103 new inscriptions (162 in all), all working on dedicated servers.
- Rarity colors at the workbench and on stands; three inscriptions get HUD icons.
- Optional Elite Creatures Reborn `Synergy`: elite stars raise drops.
- New `ecraft ecr`; `give`, `list`, `stats` show more.
- Inscription tiers count down: T1 is the strongest roll, T7 the weakest.
- Sockets: magic drops carry 0-4, the Jeweller's Chisel cuts up to 2.
- Eight family gems fill sockets with an inscription for the item's slot; a full item breaks its oldest gem.
- Eight catalysts strengthen one family's inscriptions and gems, up to +20%.
- From 0.1.0: delete or rename the main `EliteCrafting_economy.yml`; `EliteCrafting_affixes.yml` is now `EliteCrafting_inscriptions.yml`.

## 0.1.0

- First release: six rarities, Common to Mythic, by inscription count; inscription tiers 1-7 follow the biomes.
- 59 inscriptions for weapons, shields, armor, capes, utility items and tools.
- Eight stones: the five ascension stones and Lesser Growth, Turmoil and Perfection.
- Click a stone onto an item; a refused stone is not used up, and marked stones can ask first.
- Kills a player or their tame took part in drop stones and magic gear, by biome, stars and boss.
- Rarity-colored names, an inscription tooltip in three detail levels, and a ground glow on magic items.
- Magic items keep their inscriptions through a workbench upgrade.
- Two YAML files over built-in defaults, server synced and reloaded live; errors keep the previous rules.
- Console command `ecraft`.
- Your own `EliteCrafting.translations.<Language>.yml` replaces the English text.
