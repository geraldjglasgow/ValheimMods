===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Creatures spawn with mutations that change the fight: Mad ones are twice as fast on half the health, Bloated ones explode when killed, Splintering ones split in two, and a Devouring one eats the pack around it. Stars beyond vanilla's two, a world tier that climbs with each boss, and traits tamed creatures pass on. Tunable per biome, server synced.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
Creatures spawn with more stars and with mutations, named traits that change how a fight goes. Bosses take an aspect instead.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Mutations: thirteen, shown on the nameplate, such as Mad, Bloated, Devouring and Thieving.
[*]Stars: beyond vanilla's two, counted in fives; chances are set per biome.
[*]Boss aspects: one modifier per fight (Twin, Phantom and eleven more), shown at the altar; harder ones pay more.
[*]World tiers: each boss's first defeat makes stars and mutations more common everywhere.
[*]Breeding: tamed creatures pass a mutation and stars on to their young.
[*]Loot: Vanilla, Scaled, Rolled or Curated drops, and drop rules per creature.
[*]Boss damage board: who hurt the boss and how much; [b]/damage[/b] shows it again.
[*]Respawning: cleared camps, dungeons and dungeon chests can refill (off by default).
[*]Works with PackPanel, GrindstoneSkills' Husbandry and Elite Creatures Pack.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Needed on the server and every client. Install BepInExPack for Valheim, then put EliteCreaturesReborn.dll in BepInEx/plugins (or use a mod manager). Remove any other creature star mod and its configs first: this mod reads neither.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
BepInEx/config/gglasgow.elitecreaturesreborn.cfg (each player's display) and BepInEx/config/creature_rules.yml (the rules, an off switch per feature). Both apply without a restart; the server's rules bind every player. Console: [b]elite tier[/b] and [b]damage[/b] for everyone, the other [b]elite[/b] commands for admins. Every mutation, aspect, rule field and command in detail: [url=https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteCreaturesReborn/CLAUDE.md]the reference on GitHub[/url].
