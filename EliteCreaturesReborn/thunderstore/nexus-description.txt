===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Creatures spawn with mutations that change how the fight goes: Mad ones are twice as fast on half the health, Bloated ones explode when killed, Splintering ones split in two, and a Devouring one eats the pack around it until it comes for you. Stars beyond vanilla's two, a world tier that climbs with every boss you defeat, and tamed creatures that pass their traits on to their young. Tunable per biome, server synced and hot reloaded.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
Elite Creatures Reborn makes the creature in front of you worth reading before you swing at it. Creatures spawn with named, visible mutations that change how a fight goes rather than only how long it takes, and with stars beyond the two the plain game gives out. A Mad greydwarf comes at you twice as fast on half the health. A Bloated draugr takes twice the killing and then explodes in your face. A Devouring wolf eats the boars around it and grows on what it eats until it comes looking for you.

This version is a rebuild from scratch and is not an update to earlier releases: none of the old code remains. It covers mutations, stars, world tiers, breeding, loot and boss aspects. The rest of the mod's features return over the next releases.

[size=4][b]Mutations[/b][/size]
Nine, one per creature by default, each with its own colour and its own word before the creature's name.
[list]
[*][b]Mad[/b] - far faster, half health.
[*][b]Bloated[/b] - double health, explodes a second after it dies.
[*][b]Cloaked[/b] - invisible beyond 6 metres.
[*][b]Splintering[/b] - splits into two weaker copies when killed, which can split again.
[*][b]Leeching[/b] - regenerates, and heals from the damage it deals.
[*][b]Warding[/b] - reflects damage and knocks you back.
[*][b]Plated[/b] - armoured while healthy, hits harder as that armour goes.
[*][b]Miasmic[/b] - trails poison clouds; poisons players, never creatures.
[*][b]Devouring[/b] - kills creatures in one bite and keeps their health and damage, until it is big enough to hunt you.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Stars[/b][/size]
Beyond vanilla's two, counted in fives on the nameplate: a small star is one, a large star is five. A mutation on a large star is stronger. Star chances and mutation chances are set per biome, so the Meadows stay the Meadows. Bosses have their own star table, and a boss's stars are drawn under its health bar.

[size=4][b]World tiers[/b][/size]
The world starts at tier 0 and goes up one tier the first time each boss is defeated - by anyone, in any order - so the seven bosses take it to tier 7. Killing a boss again changes nothing, and every rise is announced to everyone on the server. Each tier makes stars and mutations more common in every biome while keeping each biome's character: by tier 7 the share of unstarred Meadows creatures falls from 73 in 100 to 44, five-star Plains creatures rise from 5 in 100 to 22, and every mutation chance doubles. Bosses are unaffected, and creatures already alive keep what they rolled. A world that killed bosses before the mod was installed starts at that tier. The off switch, which bosses count (modded ones too) and the boost per tier are in the rule file.

[size=4][b]Breeding[/b][/size]
Tamed creatures keep their traits and pass them on. A newborn - a pup, a piglet, a calf, or an egg and the chick that hatches from it - takes one of its parents' mutations whenever either has one, and a star count from 0 up to its stronger parent's, every count equally likely. Two plain parents have plain young, so a strong line stays strong only if you keep the good ones. Young creatures keep their traits when they grow up, and eggs keep theirs when carried and say what they will hatch. A bred mutation behaves exactly as it does in the wild - a Miasmic pet still trails poison.

[size=4][b]Boss aspects[/b][/size]
A boss never takes a mutation. It takes an [b]aspect[/b], named before the boss ("Enraged Eikthyr") and shown at its altar before you summon: hover the offering bowl for the current aspect, what it does, what it pays, and how long until it shifts. Every altar shifts to a different aspect each in-game hour, so a group can wait for the fight it wants. The Queen and console-spawned bosses roll theirs when they appear.
[list]
[*][b]Reflective[/b] - 15% of each hit you land comes back to you as true damage.
[*][b]Shielded[/b] - 30% less damage from arrows and bolts.
[*][b]Mending[/b] - regenerates 0.3% of its health every second, in combat too.
[*][b]Summoner[/b] - calls two 2-star creatures of its own biome each time it loses 33% of its health.
[*][b]Elementalist[/b] - 20% more elemental damage.
[*][b]Enraged[/b] - 20% more physical damage.
[*][b]Twin[/b] - comes as two sharing one health pool, 25% less health and damage each; both drop full loot.
[*][b]Phantom[/b] - brings four copies with 100 health and half its damage; they drop nothing and vanish when the boss dies.
[/list]
Harder aspects pay more: each has its own loot multiplier. Every number, the chances, the shift interval and each boss's summons are in the rule file.

[size=4][b]Boss damage board[/b][/size]
When a boss dies, everyone on the server sees who fought it: the boss's name and every player who hurt it, with the health each one took off it, most first, at the top of the screen for a minute. It counts what the boss actually lost - after its resistances, without the overkill - and only players count. Twin bosses are one fight and one board. Each player can turn it off or change how long it stays.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install BepInExPack for Valheim.
[*]Install Elite Creatures Reborn on the server and on every client.
[*]Start the game once. The config file gglasgow.elitecreaturesreborn.cfg and the rule file creature_rules.yml are written to BepInEx/config, both documented.
[*]Edit either while playing. Changes apply a few seconds after saving.
[/list]
For manual install, drop EliteCreaturesReborn.dll into BepInEx/plugins. The mod is standalone: it does not read the config files, save data or API of any other mod. If you used a different creature star mod before, remove it and its config files first.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
The config file carries star chances, mutation chances and mutation strength per biome, the colours, the nameplate distance, and an off switch for everything. The rule file takes anything too structured for a flat settings file. Both are written and documented on first run and both hot reload while you play.

Gameplay settings come from the server and bind every connected player; display preferences - colours, tint strength, nameplate distance, whether trait names show at all - stay with each player and are never locked.

[size=4][b]Console commands[/b][/size]
Run from the in-game console (F5).
[code]elite spawn <prefab> <stars> [mutation...]  a creature in front of you with chosen traits, bypassing every roll
                                            (a boss takes one aspect instead: elite spawn Bonemass 2 Twin)
elite inspect                               the resolved stars, mutations or aspect, and numbers for the creature under your crosshair
elite purge                                 remove the loaded creatures this mod has marked, with no drops
elite effects <text>                        list loaded effect prefabs matching the text and play one
elite reference                             write creature_reference.yml: every creature the game knows, with its drop table
elite tier                                  the world tier, what it does to the rolls, and which bosses count (any player)[/code]

[size=4][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]
Creature state travels in the world data the game already shares, so every player sees the same creature with the same traits. Install the mod on the dedicated server as well as on every client. In the console, [b]charter status[/b] shows whether the server binds your settings, [b]charter diff[/b] lists where the server's values differ from your own file, and [b]charter versions[/b] lists the mods on both sides.
