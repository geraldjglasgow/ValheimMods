===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Creatures spawn with more stars and mutations that change the fight: Mad ones are twice as fast on half the health, Bloated ones explode, Frostbound ones leave slippery ice, Cloning ones hide behind a decoy. Bosses take an aspect, shown at the altar. Difficulty from Easy to Extreme, a death recap, traits tamed creatures pass on. Server synced.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
Creatures spawn with more stars and mutations, traits that change how a fight goes; bosses take an aspect instead.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Difficulty: Easy to Extreme; new biomes start gentle, cleared ones keep hardening.
[*]Stars: beyond vanilla's two, up to eight on Extreme, or off for another mod's.
[*]World tiers: each boss's first defeat makes stars and mutations more common.
[*]Mutations: one per creature, named and coloured on the nameplate (below).
[*]Boss aspects: one per fight, shown at the altar with the boss's stars (below), or chosen there with E; each defeat makes a boss likelier to have stars next time.
[*]Taming: young inherit a mutation and stars; tames can keep only the game's stars, or no mutations.
[*]Loot: four drop modes, rules per creature; bosses drop trophies and materials for every player.
[*]Boss damage board: who did how much damage.
[*]Boss hints: four bosses point the way to the Bog Witch, the ancient forge, Haldor and Hildir, until you have found them.
[*]Compendium: pages in the game's compendium on the difficulty, stars, mutations and boss aspects, with your server's numbers.
[*]Death recap: [b]F10[/b] replays the seconds before each death, hit by hit.
[*]Respawning: cleared camps, dungeons and dungeon chests can refill (off by default).
[*]Raids: stake gold in the Raiders Chest to call a raid and win it back with more (below).
[*]Horde (off by default): more creatures in the wild, each weaker; the extra creatures pay no extra loot.
[*]Hel's Arena (off by default): an outdoor arena 130 m across, reached through Helgrind, Hel's gate, under a cavern roof and ringed by a black lake that kills; three Bat-fiends guard it on the first visit and drop runes. Endless waves starting at your gear's biome and a boss every fifth; souls for every kill, spent at Hel's Anvil to mend your gear between waves; rewarded with Forge of Potential idols, coins, skill XP and (with EliteCrafting) runes and essence by how far you get. Subject to change without warning: it works and is tested, but its final shape is still being decided.
[*]Works with PackPanel, GrindstoneSkills' Husbandry and Elite Creatures Pack.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Mutations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Mad: far faster, half health.
[*]Bloated: double health, explodes after it dies.
[*]Cloaked: invisible until you are within 10 m.
[*]Splintering: splits into two weaker copies when killed.
[*]Leeching: regenerates and heals from the damage it deals.
[*]Warding: reflects part of each hit and knocks you back.
[*]Plated: armoured while healthy, hits harder as the armour breaks.
[*]Miasmic: trails poison clouds.
[*]Devouring: eats weaker creatures and keeps their health and damage.
[*]Thieving: steals an item with each hit; kill it to get them back.
[*]Gilded: runs from you; drops triple loot and a purse of coins.
[*]Blinking: reappears behind you every 30 seconds.
[*]Relentless: keeps chasing until you are 150 m away; sneaking does not hide you.
[*]Juggernaut: never staggers or is knocked back.
[*]Screecher: a big hit makes it shriek, deafening you and stopping your magic.
[*]Frostbound: a chilling aura and slippery ice trails; frost heals it.
[*]Mudbound: leaves slowing mud where it walks.
[*]Corrodent: wears your armour down three times as fast.
[*]Cloning: leaves a harmless decoy and fights on unseen.
[*]Flamebound: leaves burning ground that sets you on fire.
[*]Piercing, Howling and Binding (off by default): hits ignore 15% of your armour; calls nearby creatures to fight you; leaves roots that hold you for a second.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Boss aspects[/b][/size]
The altar shows the aspect before you offer and shifts every 15 seconds; press E on the bowl to choose the aspect and stars yourself, or leave either on Random. About one fight in five has none; every aspect pays extra loot.
[list]
[*]Reflective: part of each hit comes back to you.
[*]Shielded: takes less damage from arrows and bolts.
[*]Mending: regenerates during the fight.
[*]Summoner: calls creatures of its biome as it loses health.
[*]Elementalist / Enraged: more elemental / physical damage.
[*]Twin: two bosses sharing one health pool.
[*]Tethered: two linked bosses that attack faster the further apart their health is.
[*]Phantom: splits into copies and hides among them.
[*]Adaptive: resists whichever damage type hits it most.
[*]Fixated: marks one player and hits them harder.
[*]Stormbound: lightning strikes circles under every player.
[*]Gravitic: drags everyone in, then slams.
[*]Colossal: bigger and tougher; its shockwaves knock you down.
[*]Brutal: heavy blows throw you 20 m.
[*]Nightfall: a storming night with tornadoes that hunt you.
[*]Echoing: a white ghost of the boss repeats its every move 15 seconds later, blows included.
[*]Portalbound: the Elder and Bonemass throw through portals.
[*]Bountiful: two more aspects at once, and all their loot.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Raids: the Raiders Chest[/b][/size]
The game's own raids still come as usual. The Raiders Chest calls one on your own base, for gold.
[list]
[*][b]Build it:[/b] hammer, Misc tab, at a workbench: 10 Wood, 4 Leather Scraps, 1 Hard Antler. It holds only coins.
[*][b]Sound the raid:[/b] fill it with coins and press [b]Shift + E[/b]. Its hover shows what the coins would make of a raid now, for example "1,000 coins - Deadly raid (coins back x2, drops x5)".
[*][b]Where and when:[/b] the chest must stand in a base (3 base pieces such as a workbench, fire or bed within 20 m). One raid a day per chest (chests within 100 m share the wait), and none within 200 m of another raid, the game's or a chest's.
[*][b]Gold against gear:[/b] sounding takes every coin in the chest. The raid's heat is those coins against a fair stake for the strongest player within 96 m (the best metal they have ever held or the armour they wear, whichever is higher): 100 coins for leather and bone, 200 bronze, 400 iron, 600 silver, 900 black metal, 1,300 Mistlands, 1,800 Flametal.
[/list]

Heat (coins divided by the fair stake):
[list]
[*][b]Trivial[/b] (under 0.5): fewer raiders (x0.6); coins back x1; drops x0.1 to x0.5.
[*][b]Fair[/b] (0.5 to 1.5): the normal count; coins back x1.1; drops x1.
[*][b]Hard[/b] (1.5 to 3): x1.5 raiders, +1 star, a third mutated; coins back x1.25; drops x2.
[*][b]Brutal[/b] (3 to 6): x2 raiders, +2 stars, half mutated; coins back x1.5; drops x3.5.
[*][b]Deadly[/b] (6 and up): x2.5 raiders, +3 stars, 9 in 10 mutated; coins back x2; drops x5.
[/list]

The raid:
[list]
[*]A 20 second countdown, then three waves of one of the game's raids your world has unlocked, each from one side, 40 to 60 m out: half the game's count, then two thirds, then all of it, times the heat's raiders and a quarter more for each player past the first. At most 20 alive at once.
[*]Raiders roll stars and mutations as their biome does, plus the heat's. A Warlord leads the last wave: its toughest creature, with two more stars.
[*]Raiders hunt players first. With no one to fight, half go for the Raiders Chest and half for your chests and crafting stations, breaking through whatever stands in the way.
[*]The coins back are split among every raider (the Warlord carries a quarter); each drops its share as it dies, with its own loot times the heat's drops. A stronger player arriving mid-raid lowers the drops to match their gear.
[*]Players within 96 m see the raid in the event bar under the minimap: wave, raiders left and time left.
[/list]

How it ends:
[list]
[*][b]Won:[/b] the last wave is dead; every coin is back, with the extra.
[*][b]Robbed:[/b] the Raiders Chest is broken; the raiders walk off with their coins.
[*][b]Stopped:[/b] hold [b]E[/b] on the chest for 3 seconds; every raider dies and drops nothing, coins included.
[*][b]Abandoned:[/b] no player within 96 m for 60 seconds, a logout or a server restart; the raiders walk off with their coins.
[*][b]Timed out:[/b] 15 minutes after the start; the raiders walk off with their coins.
[/list]
The chest stays locked during the raid, and whatever raiders dropped before the end is yours to keep. [b]Raiders Chest[/b] in section [b]11 - Raids[/b] (on by default, set by the server) takes the chest out of the hammer.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Needed on the server and every client. Install BepInExPack for Valheim, then put EliteCreaturesReborn.dll in BepInEx/plugins (or use a mod manager). Remove other star mods, or set creature stars: false: their configs are not read.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
BepInEx/config/gglasgow.elitecreaturesreborn.cfg (each player's own settings) and BepInEx/config/creature_rules.yml (the rules). Both apply without a restart; the server's rules bind every player. Console: [b]elite tier[/b], [b]damage[/b] and [b]deaths[/b] for everyone, the other [b]elite[/b] commands for admins. In detail: [url=https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteCreaturesReborn/CLAUDE.md]the reference on GitHub[/url]. Translate: save a copy of [url=https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteCreaturesReborn/EliteCreaturesReborn/translations/English.yml]English.yml[/url] as EliteCreaturesReborn.translations.<Language>.yml in BepInEx/config.
