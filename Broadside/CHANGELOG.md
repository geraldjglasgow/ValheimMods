# Changelog

## 0.7.0

- A ship that sinks or is destroyed gives back what it cost in floating crates.
- `ShowShipPanel` hides the ship panel on your own screen.
- Fixed: a ship left behind could no longer be broken up ("Everyone must leave the ship first").
- Fixed: the ship panel stayed on screen after leaving the ship.
- Fixed: players near a hammock aboard counted as on the ship.
- Fixed: ShipConfig's health and Ashlands settings no longer revert on Broadside ships; the helm card shows them.
- Trawl: the catch lands dead and can be picked up.

## 0.6.0

- Dock tool: level piers on pilings in wood, core wood or fine wood, branching off existing docks.
- 34 dockside decorations, and an auto-decorate tool that fills an area round docks and paths.
- Hammock: a bed built on a launched ship; your spawn point sails with it.
- Trawl net winch: cast a net astern while sailing, then haul the catch onto the deck.
- The cargo crane lifts every crate, onto and off ships and the game's cart, which now carries crates.
- A crate dropped from over 2 m breaks and spills.
- Cannons keep the aim their gunner left; Shift snaps a cannon into a gunport.
- Ships sail faster: a big, well-rigged ship is about as fast as a karve.
- ShipConfig's `KW_ship` settings and other mods' sailing bonuses now apply to Broadside ships.
- Fixed: a crate set on a ship's deck no longer holds the ship still.

## 0.5.0

- Storage crates: small and large rope-lashed crates, and small and large slatted crates, each slatted crate marked its own way.
- Cannonball pile and black powder pile: ammunition stores that fill up visibly as you stock them.
- The captain's broadside reloads from the piles on his ship first, then from his pack.
- Crates and ammunition piles can be built on a launched ship.
- Cargo crane: a land crane that lifts its cargo crate, its boom reaching out 12 m.

## 0.4.1

- Fixed: the Storm Petrel's hold was too low to walk in; it is now 2.1 m high.
- Fixed: the Wayfinder's hold was too low for anyone; its hatch is gone.
- Ready-made ships you have not changed now update with the mod. Ships already launched keep their old layout.
- Fixed: a huge inventory (unlimited weight mods) no longer makes every ship overloaded; a pack counts at most 450 kg.
- The dock pieces are now named "Dry dock: keel block guide" and "Dry dock: keel block".

## 0.4.0

- `AllowBuildingOnShips` (off): build any piece on a launched ship, not only cannons; it sails with the ship.
- The hammer's Categories tab has a Broadside entry under Transportation holding every Broadside piece.
- Players now need the server's Broadside version to join.

## 0.3.4

- Fixed: ships loading in no longer stall a frame on their holes, rig damage and sails; the work is spread out.

## 0.3.3

- Fixed: a saved ship's whole cost now counts items in nearby chests when a craft-from-containers mod is installed.
- A saved ship's hammer tooltip now lists its whole cost first.
- Fixed: players with a taller or wider body (a race mod) land where they fit after a climb.

## 0.3.2

- Fixed: ships no longer sink by one end or roll over when you leave the area and come back.

## 0.3.1

- Fixed: launched ships sail and steer again after the game's latest patch.

## 0.3.0

- The small iron ship cannon (14 iron, 22 ancient bark) is in the hammer, firing iron cannonballs.
- Iron cannonballs: 1 iron makes 4 at the forge (level 2).
- A falling mast creaks and splinters as it leans and lands with a timber crash.

## 0.2.0

- Logging off aboard a ship puts you back on the same spot when you log in, not inside the hull.
- A mast shot through high up drops only what is above the break; the sails below stay set.
- A shot-down mast takes twice as long to fall.
- Fixed: lag spikes when taking up the hammer, previewing a saved ship and launching or loading ships.
- Fixed: the broadside key (U) did nothing after using the console.

## 0.1.1

- Fixed: climbing a ladder onto a cabin roof no longer drags you back down to the deck.
- Fixed: climbing masts and boarding ladders no longer pulls you back toward where you started the climb.

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- Dry dock and hull tools: design a ship part by part, edit and undo it, in Meadows and Black Forest woods.
- Blueprint table: save, build back and share ships, with speed, stability and cost figures.
- Saved ships built on the water from the hammer: they float, sail by the wind and steer by tiller or wheel.
- A bronze ship cannon, stone cannonballs and black powder; the broadside on U, shot arcs on J.
- Hull holes, flooding, rig damage, wooden repair kits and editing at sea.
- Ship panel: speed, top speed, explore radius, health and reloads.
- 11 ready-made ships.
