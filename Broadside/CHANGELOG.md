# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Fixed: climbing a ladder onto a cabin roof no longer drags you back down to the deck.
- Fixed: climbing masts and boarding ladders no longer pulls you back toward where you started the climb.

## 0.1.0

- First release.
- Dry dock and hull tools: design a ship part by part, edit and undo it, in Meadows and Black Forest woods.
- Blueprint table: save, build back and share ships, with speed, stability and cost figures.
- Saved ships built on the water from the hammer: they float, sail by the wind and steer by tiller or wheel.
- A bronze ship cannon, stone cannonballs and black powder; the broadside on U, shot arcs on J.
- Hull holes, flooding, rig damage, wooden repair kits and editing at sea.
- Ship panel: speed, top speed, explore radius, health and reloads.
- 11 ready-made ships.
