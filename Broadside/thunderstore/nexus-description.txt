===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Design and build your own ships, from a one-man skiff to a 50 m pirate galleon: keel, ribs, planking, decks, cabins, masts and sails on a dry dock, then launch from your hammer and sail. Bronze ship cannons fire broadsides that hole hulls and sink ships. 11 ready-made ships included. Meadows and Black Forest for now.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
Design and build your own ships, from a one-man skiff to a 50 m pirate galleon. Lay a keel on a dry dock, raise ribs, plank the hull, add decks, cabins, masts and sails, then launch it from your hammer and sail. Arm it with ship cannons and fight: shots punch holes, the sea floods in, and wrecked ships sink.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Dry dock: keel block guides and keel blocks to build on; designing is free.
[*]Hull tools: keel, rib, planking, deck, railing, ladder, cabin, masts and sails, bowsprit, post caps, doors and windows, each sized in a small window. Undo with Ctrl + Z, and any part can be edited after it is built.
[*]Meadows and Black Forest woods and fittings, a rudder for each, and raw hide sails.
[*]Blueprint table: save the ship on the dock, build a saved one back onto it, share designs with the server, and see a ship's speed, stability, weight and cost before you build it.
[*]Launch from the hammer: every saved ship is built on the water near a workbench, for what its parts cost.
[*]Real sailing: the ship floats on its own hull; square sails want the wind behind, gaff sails and jibs sail closer to it.
[*]Steering by tiller, or a captain's wheel with working steering gear; anchor windlass, hatches, crow's nests, pennants.
[*]Ship panel: speed, top speed for the wind and sails you have now, explore radius, health and your guns' reloads.
[*]A bronze ship cannon, built on a launched ship (the only piece built aboard), firing stone cannonballs with black powder.
[*]Broadside from the helm: U fires the side you look toward, J shows the shot arcs.
[*]Battle damage: holes through the hull, flooding, bailing, holed sails and falling masts; wooden repair kits patch leaks at sea and the hammer mends the rest.
[*]Edit at sea: railings, ladders, doors, windows, masts and sails can be changed while the ship is afloat.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Ready-made ships[/b][/size]
Found in the hammer under "Ship: Ready-made/...". Gunports come empty: build cannons on the ship once it is launched.
[list]
[*]Black_Gull: 50 m pirate galleon, two gun decks, 40 gunports, three masts.
[*]Little_Gull: half-size galleon, 12 gunports.
[*]Storm_Petrel: two-masted brig-of-war, 18 gunports.
[*]Wayfinder: explorer's schooner with a crow's nest, 6 gunports.
[*]Merchant_Cog: tall-sided trader with a cargo hold.
[*]Hearth_Barge: a floating home with a long deckhouse.
[*]Sea_Raven: a Viking longship.
[*]War_Raven: the longship with three lidded gunports a side.
[*]Fjord_Skiff: a small coastal boat.
[*]Fjord_Wasp: the skiff with two gunports a side.
[*]Skerry_Sprite: a tiny, fast sloop for one.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Needed on every client and the server. Install BepInExPack Valheim, then put [b]Broadside.dll[/b] in [b]BepInEx/plugins[/b], or install with r2modman or the Thunderstore app.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
No settings. Saved ships are JSON files in [b]BepInEx/config/Broadside.Ships[/b], sail pictures PNG files in [b]BepInEx/config/Broadside.Sails[/b].
