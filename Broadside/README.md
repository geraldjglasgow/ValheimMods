# Broadside

Design and build your own ships, from a one-man skiff to a 50 m pirate galleon. Lay a keel on a dry dock, raise ribs,
plank the hull, add decks, cabins, masts and sails, then launch it from your hammer and sail. Arm it with ship cannons
and fight: shots punch holes, the sea floods in, and wrecked ships sink.

[![Broadside - Ship Cannons](https://img.youtube.com/vi/8zDX1wglYUQ/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8zDX1wglYUQ)

Watch the ship cannons video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8zDX1wglYUQ

## Features
- Dry dock: keel block guides and keel blocks to build on; designing is free.
- Hull tools: keel, rib, planking, deck, railing, ladder, cabin, masts and sails, bowsprit, post caps, doors and windows,
  each sized in a small window. Undo with Ctrl + Z, and any part can be edited after it is built.
- Meadows and Black Forest woods and fittings, a rudder for each, and raw hide sails.
- Blueprint table: save the ship on the dock, build a saved one back onto it, share designs with the server, and see a
  ship's speed, stability, weight and cost before you build it.
- Launch from the hammer: every saved ship is built on the water near a workbench, for what its parts cost.
- Real sailing: the ship floats on its own hull; square sails want the wind behind, gaff sails and jibs sail closer to it.
- Steering by tiller, or a captain's wheel with working steering gear; anchor windlass, hatches, crow's nests, pennants.
- Ship panel: speed, top speed for the wind and sails you have now, explore radius, health and your guns' reloads.
- Log off aboard a ship and you log back in on the same spot on deck.
- Ship cannons, built on a launched ship (the only piece built aboard): bronze with stone shot, or iron with iron shot.
- Broadside from the helm: U fires the side you look toward, J shows the shot arcs.
- Battle damage: holes through the hull, flooding, bailing, holed sails and falling masts; wooden repair kits patch
  leaks at sea and the hammer mends the rest.
- Edit at sea: railings, ladders, doors, windows, masts and sails can be changed while the ship is afloat.

## Ready-made ships
Found in the hammer under "Ship: Ready-made/...":
- Black_Gull: 50 m pirate galleon, two gun decks, 40 gunports, three masts.
- Little_Gull: half-size galleon, 12 gunports.
- Storm_Petrel: two-masted brig-of-war, 18 gunports.
- Wayfinder: explorer's schooner with a crow's nest, 6 gunports.
- Merchant_Cog: tall-sided trader with a cargo hold.
- Hearth_Barge: a floating home with a long deckhouse.
- Sea_Raven: a Viking longship.
- War_Raven: the longship with three lidded gunports a side.
- Fjord_Skiff: a small coastal boat.
- Fjord_Wasp: the skiff with two gunports a side.
- Skerry_Sprite: a tiny, fast sloop for one.

Gunports come empty: build cannons on the ship once it is launched.

## Install
Needed on every client and the server. Install with r2modman or the Thunderstore app, or put `Broadside.dll` in
`BepInEx/plugins`.

## Configuration
No settings. Saved ships are JSON files in `BepInEx/config/Broadside.Ships`, sail pictures PNG files in
`BepInEx/config/Broadside.Sails`.
