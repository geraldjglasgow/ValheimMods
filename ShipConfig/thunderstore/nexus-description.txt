===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
Configure every ship in Valheim: health, speed, steering, damage and build cost, for vanilla and modded ships. Changes apply at once, also to ships already built. Server synced.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
Configure every ship in Valheim: health, speed, steering, damage and build cost, for vanilla and modded ships.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Every ship gets its own settings, defaulting to its vanilla values (below).
[*]Global multipliers on top: health, sail force, paddle force, turning, damage taken and build cost.
[*]Modded ships get settings too (any ship with a Ship and a WearNTear component).
[*]Changes apply at once, to new ships and to ships already in the world.
[*]AshlandsOceanDamage off lets ships sail the Ashlands ocean unharmed (this skips the Drakkar's progression gate).
[*]A small ship panel under the minimap while aboard (below).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Per ship[/b][/size]
Raft, Karve, Longship, Drakkar and every modded ship each get:
[list]
[*][b]Health[/b]: the ship's maximum health.
[*][b]Sail force[/b]: top speed under sail; its height sets how far the ship heels.
[*][b]Paddle force[/b]: speed while paddling.
[*][b]Steering[/b]: rudder speed and turning force, under sail and paddling.
[*][b]Drag and damping[/b]: top speed, sideways slide and how steady the deck is.
[*][b]Damage[/b]: from every hit, rough seas, capsizing and the Ashlands ocean, or none at all.
[*][b]Weather wear[/b]: health lost in rain and under water, on or off.
[*][b]Build cost[/b]: a multiplier on every material.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Ship panel[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Speed[/b]: how fast the ship moves, in km/h.
[*][b]Speed multiplier[/b]: top speed against a vanilla ship of its kind.
[*][b]Explore radius[/b]: how far around you the map uncovers as you sail.
[*][b]Sailing abilities[/b]: with GrindstoneSkills, each ability and its cooldown; hover to read what it does.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Needed on the server and every client. Install BepInExPack for Valheim, then put ShipConfig.dll in BepInEx/plugins (or use a mod manager).

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
BepInEx/config/com.ShipConfig.cfg; the ship entries appear after the first world load. Every setting is described in the file and applies without a restart; the server's values bind every player (Lock Configuration). Console: [b]charter status[/b] shows whether the server binds your settings.
