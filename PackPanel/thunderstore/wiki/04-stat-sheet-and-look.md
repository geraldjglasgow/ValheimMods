# Stat Sheet and Look

## Stat sheet

The Gear tab lists your stats as the game counts them, with everything you wear and every effect on you. Hover a line to see what makes it up. Scroll with the mouse wheel.

| Line | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Health, Stamina, Eitr | Your maximums |
| Armour | Your armour |
| Weight | Carried / carry limit, red when over |
| Movement | Speed change from gear and effects |
| Durability | The most worn item you have equipped, red under 25% |
| Weapons and shields in hand | Damage per hit with your skill and effects, attack cost, knockback, backstab; block armour, block force and parry bonus |
| Resistances | Each damage type you resist (green) or are weak to (red) |
| Gear bonuses | Every other modifier from your gear, such as stamina use |
| Epic Loot | With Epic Loot installed: your active magic effects and their totals |

## Stat boxes

- A column between the grid and the slot panel shows your armour, your weight (carried over carry limit, flashing red when over) and boxes from other mods, such as Elite Creatures Reborn's world tier.
- `Weight Under Minimap` shows the same boxes in a column right of the minimap. The minimap moves a little left to make room.

## Look

All look settings are your own.

- `Brown Style` gives the inventory PackPanel's look; off, it uses the game's wood.
- `Panel Theme`:
  - Timber: wood wallpaper with chopped, bevelled edges, also on crafting, the character panel and the game's other menus.
  - Brown: plain dark brown panels with a bronze frame.
- `Night Shade`: the panels darken at night and in dark weather, as the game's own do.
- `Slot Labels` shows each empty slot's icon and name.
