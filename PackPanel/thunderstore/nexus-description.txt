===== SUMMARY (plain text, one paragraph) =====
A bigger, organised inventory: an 8 x 5 grid, labelled slots for armour, a backpack, utilities, a trinket, food, meads and ammo on Gear and Consumables tabs with a stat sheet, a coin purse, a key ring, a tacklebox, eight craftable backpacks worn on your back, and stat boxes by the minimap. Server synced and lockable; works alone or with OpenKeep.

===== DESCRIPTION (BBCode) =====
A bigger, better organised inventory, with labelled slots always on screen.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]A bigger grid (up to 12 x 10), with Base Carry Weight and Keep Slots On Death.
[*]Gear tab: armour, a backpack, worn utilities, a trinket and a stat sheet.
[*]Consumables tab: food, mead and ammo, with keys to eat and drink (Z, B).
[*]A coin purse, a key ring and four craftable tackleboxes for bait.
[*]Eight craftable backpacks you wear, for more slots and carry weight.
[*]Armour and weight beside the minimap; a brown or timber look.
[*]With OpenKeep 1.8.0 or later, its buttons join the inventory and its quick stack and sort skip the slots.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Needed on the server and every client. Install BepInExPack for Valheim, then put PackPanel.dll in BepInEx/plugins (or use a mod manager).
[list]
[*]Not compatible with mods that resize the inventory or add equipment slots, such as ExtraSlots.
[*]Removing the mod drops or loses what lies outside the game's grid, every backpack and tacklebox, and keys above one per stack: empty them first.
[*]Lowering Inventory Width or Key Stack can lose items; fewer slots or taking off a pack drops what no longer fits.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
BepInEx/config/milkyteam.packpanel.cfg, plus PackPanel.Backpacks.yml and PackPanel.Tackleboxes.yml ([url=https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/PackPanel/CLAUDE.md#backpack-and-tacklebox-yaml-files]format[/url]). Every setting is described in the file and applies without a restart; the server's values bind every player, except your look and keys.
