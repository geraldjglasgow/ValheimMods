# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- Fixed: translated text no longer turns back to English with OdinHorse or similar mods installed.

## 0.2.0

- Translations: every word you read is in `translations/English.yml`; a `Hearthhold.translations.<Language>.yml` in `BepInEx/config` translates it.

## 0.1.1

- Fixed: players on a slow connection with many mods are no longer refused when joining a server.

## 0.1.0

- First release: stars on forage, honey, sap, crops, meat, fish, dishes and meads, shown bronze, silver and gold.
- Starred food gives more and lasts longer; starred meads are stronger or last longer.
- Daily Fortune, professions at level 100, the Shipping Crate, trader friendship and the Aging Cask.
