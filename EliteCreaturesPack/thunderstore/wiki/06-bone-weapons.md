# Bone Weapons

Bone weapons made at the workbench from what the [Skeletons](wiki:Skeletons) drop.

**With EliteCrafting:** every weapon here is item level 2 (Black Forest) and can become magic, as the game's weapon of
its class (the Bone Dagger a knife, the Executioner's Greataxe a battleaxe). Spines, arrows and bolts stack and never do.

**Spine:** a crafting material that weighs and stacks like bone fragments. Arsenal skeletons and crossbowmen drop
one, one kill in ten.

## The seven bone weapons

Made at a **level 2 workbench**. Each handles like the bronze-age weapon of its class, at 0.85 times its damage. The
spine is paid once; upgrades cost bone fragments only.

| Item | Like | Damage | Per upgrade | Recipe | Each upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bone Dagger | Copper knife | 10.2 slash, 10.2 pierce | +0.85 each | 8 Bone fragments | 4 Bone fragments |
| Bone Sword | Bronze sword | 29.75 slash | +5.1 | 1 Spine, 10 Bone fragments | 5 Bone fragments |
| Bone Axe | Bronze axe | 34 slash, 34 chop | +4.25, +2.55 | 1 Spine, 10 Bone fragments | 5 Bone fragments |
| Bone Mace | Bronze mace | 29.75 blunt | +5.1 | 1 Spine, 12 Bone fragments | 6 Bone fragments |
| Bone Spear | Bronze spear | 29.75 pierce | +5.1 | 1 Spine, 10 Bone fragments | 5 Bone fragments |
| Bone Atgeir | Bronze atgeir | 38.25 pierce | +5.1 | 2 Spines, 16 Bone fragments | 8 Bone fragments |
| Bone Bow | Fine bow | 27.2 pierce | +2.55 | 1 Spine, 12 Bone fragments | 6 Bone fragments |

**Bone Arrows:** 20 for 8 Bone fragments at a level 2 workbench; 24 pierce (wood 22, flint 27).

## Bone Crossbow

A light crossbow that works like the game's crossbows (Crossbows skill, bolts as ammo), with no recoil.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Made at | Level 2 workbench |
| Recipe | 1 Spine, 10 Wood (+5 per upgrade), 12 Bone fragments (+6), 4 Leather scraps (+2) |
| Damage | 30 blunt, +4 per upgrade, plus the bolt's |
| Reload | 2.3 s, down to half with Crossbows skill |
| Quality levels | 3 |
| Durability | 100, +50 per level |
| Weight | 2 |

**Blunted Bone Bolts:** 20 for 8 Bone fragments at the same workbench level. They add 27 blunt, fit any crossbow, and
crossbowmen drop them.

## Executioner's Greataxe

A two-handed axe like the Battleaxe, made from the Crypt Executioner's axehead.

- **Recipe:** 1 Executioner's axehead, 1 Spine, 10 Bone fragments at a level 3 workbench. No upgrades.
- **Damage:** 55 slash, 49 chop. Chops trees like a bronze axe.
- **Combo:** a slash, a spin that **hits all round you**, then an overhead blow.

The seven weapons, the spine and the arrows have no settings.
