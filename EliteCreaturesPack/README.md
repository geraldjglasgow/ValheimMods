# Elite Creatures Pack

New creatures for Valheim, each with its own fight, and bone weapons made from what they drop.

[![Elite Creatures Pack trailer](https://img.youtube.com/vi/75P_wgenA2w/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=75P_wgenA2w)

Watch the trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=75P_wgenA2w

## Features
- Creatures: a mimic, a slinger, a mountain giant, a sea boss and new crypt skeletons (below).
- Bone weapons: every weapon the skeletons carry, made at the workbench (below).
- Kraken loot: a shield that bites back and a food better than serpent meat.
- With Elite Creatures Reborn (optional) they roll stars and mutations, a dormant mimic stays disguised, and the
  kraken needs 3.12.0 or later.
- With EliteCrafting (optional) every creature drops runes and the bone weapons roll inscriptions.

## Creatures
- Crypt Mimic (burial chambers, sunken crypts): a crypt chest that bites its opener, then lunges; roll away.
- Greydwarf Slinger (Black Forest): keeps its distance and shoots stones; never claws.
- Rime Giant (Mountains, very rare): an ice-plated troll asleep by day; fire breaks its plates, its slam sends an
  avalanche.
- Kraken (deep ocean, rare boss): hunts ships and holds them; tentacle slams, then bites and ink at the rail.
- Arsenal skeletons (Black Forest): Cutthroat, Swordsman, Axeman, Bonebreaker, Spearman, Halberdier and Bowman, each
  with a bone weapon.
- Skeleton Crossbowman (Black Forest): a fast bolt, then a long reload; close in while it reloads.
- Crypt Executioner (burial chambers, mini boss): a bone greataxe that slams, spins and flies, raising skeletons where
  it lands.

## Weapons and gear
- Spine: dropped by arsenal skeletons, the key material of the bone weapons.
- Bone Dagger, Sword, Axe, Mace, Spear and Atgeir: handle like their bronze-age kin.
- Bone Bow and Bone Arrows: a bow like the fine bow, arrows from bone fragments.
- Bone Crossbow: a light crossbow with a quick reload and no recoil.
- Blunted Bone Bolts: blunt bolts for any crossbow; crossbowmen drop them too.
- Executioner's Greataxe: made from the Executioner's axehead; its combo spins to hit all round you.
- Kraken shield: better than the serpent scale shield, and a parry makes its beak bite back.
- Cooked kraken tentacle: cooks like serpent meat, more health and stamina for longer.
- Paws of the Bear (the game's bear claws): each punch is three fast swipes, about a third of the damage each.
- Bone Ballista: a small ballista with spine arms that you hold and aim yourself (90 degrees of turn); it reloads
  from Bone Missiles in your pack.

## Install
Needed on the server and every client. Install with r2modman or the Thunderstore app, or put `EliteCreaturesPack.dll`
in `BepInEx/plugins`. Uninstalling deletes the mod's creatures and items, and a crypt chest that became a mimic stays
gone. On macOS the models may not show. Texts are English only for now.

## Configuration
`BepInEx/config/com.EliteCreaturesPack.cfg`. Every setting is described in the file and reloads while the game runs;
the server's values bind every player. Console: `charter status`. Epic Loot needs an `ECP_CryptMimic` entry to give
the mimic magic items. Prefab names: https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteCreaturesPack/CLAUDE.md
