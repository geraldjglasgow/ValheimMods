# Elite Creatures Pack

New creatures for Valheim, each with its own fight, and bone weapons to make.

## Features
- Crypt Mimic: some crypt chests bite whoever opens them.
- Greydwarf Slinger: a greydwarf that shoots stones from afar.
- Rime Giant: a rare ice-plated mountain troll; fire breaks its plates.
- Kraken: a sea boss that hunts ships; its beak makes a shield that bites back.
- Skeleton Crossbowman: a skeleton whose bone crossbow reloads after every shot.
- Arsenal skeletons: skeletons with bone daggers, swords, axes, maces, spears, atgeirs and bows.
- Crypt Executioner: a burial chamber mini boss with a bone greataxe.
- Skeleton arsenal: the skeletons' bone weapons, crossbow and greataxe, to make at the workbench.
- With Elite Creatures Reborn (optional) they roll stars and mutations, a dormant mimic stays disguised, and the
  kraken needs 3.12.0 or later.
- With EliteCrafting (optional) every creature drops runes and the bone weapons roll inscriptions.

## Install
Needed on the server and every client. Install with r2modman or the Thunderstore app, or put `EliteCreaturesPack.dll`
in `BepInEx/plugins`. Uninstalling deletes the mod's creatures and items, and a crypt chest that became a mimic stays
gone. On macOS the models may not show. Texts are English only for now.

## Configuration
`BepInEx/config/com.EliteCreaturesPack.cfg`. Every setting is described in the file and reloads while the game runs;
the server's values bind every player. Console: `charter status`. Epic Loot needs an `ECP_CryptMimic` entry to give
the mimic magic items. Prefab names: https://github.com/geraldjglasgow/ValheimMods/blob/main/EliteCreaturesPack/CLAUDE.md
